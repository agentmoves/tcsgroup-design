**StatBlock** — big brand figure with an uppercase label (the site's counters).

```jsx
<StatBlock value="4,500" suffix="+" label="Rental Units Managed" />
<StatBlock prefix="$" value="500" suffix="M" label="2023 Closed" tone="salmon" />
<StatBlock value="80" suffix="+" label="Local Agents" tone="light" />
```

`tone="light"` for dark/navy backgrounds. `align` left or center.
