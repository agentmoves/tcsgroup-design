**SectionHeading** — salmon eyebrow + display title + optional description. Opens marketing sections.

```jsx
<SectionHeading eyebrow="Our Clients"
  title="Consistently rated 5 stars for our service"
  description="Known for our local expertise. Made famous by our results." />
<SectionHeading align="center" tone="light" title="TCS Group Family" />
```

`align` left/center, `tone="light"` on dark backgrounds.
