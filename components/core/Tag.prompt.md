**Tag** — status pill / category chip.

```jsx
<Tag tone="salmon">For Sale</Tag>
<Tag tone="sky">Condos</Tag>
<Tag tone="navy" dot>Featured</Tag>
<Tag tone="outline" size="sm">Rittenhouse</Tag>
```

Tones: `navy | salmon | sky | slate | outline | success`. `dot` adds a leading status dot. Two sizes.
