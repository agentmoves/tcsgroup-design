My Story
Why I built the Burn-to-Earn™ Formula

When my wife and I got married, we started out renting like many newlyweds. But after two years of paying $1,200 in rent monthly, I felt like we were burning money. Something had to change.

As immigrants without permanent status, we were hesitant to buy. We didn't have a 20% down payment, and committing to a mortgage felt overwhelming. What if we lost our jobs? The fear of making the wrong decision held us back.

But as I learned more about real estate, I discovered we didn't need as much upfront as we'd thought. That knowledge gave me the confidence to buy my first home — an investment that changed my life.

Since then, I've shared what I learned, helping hundreds of renters buy their first homes.

"It felt like we were setting twelve $100 bills on fire. Then the first of the month comes, and we do it again. We did that every month for two years."

The math equation that matters
